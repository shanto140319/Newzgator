# NewsGator logo

## Direction

An English horizontal wordmark with a folded-news N symbol. Multiple editorial sources converge into one reading destination. Navy `#0C1C34` and red `#C83018` match the website palette. Transparent background; no tagline.

## Research

- [Flipboard brand guidelines](https://cdn.flipboard.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/Flipboard-Basic-Brand-Guidelines-2018.pdf): horizontal primary logo, limited palette, protected clear space.
- [Google News icon guidance](https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-news/visual-identity/visual-identity/Icon-visual-identity/): distinguish the full logo from icon-only usage.

These inform general design principles, not copying the brands' artwork.

## Generation

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Raster PNG concept, not an editable vector master.
